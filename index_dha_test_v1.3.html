Official DHA PMU Guidelines (2026)

Scope of Practice

The Dubai Health Authority mandates strict licensing for PMU artists (Microblading, Lip Blush, Eyeliner). Professionals must operate under aesthetic/medical facility licenses and adhere to strict infection control.

Exam Specifications

  • System: Prometric Computer-Based Test.
  • Language: English.
  • Duration & Limit: 50 questions, 30 minutes. 60% passing score (Max 20 errors).

Core Topics Tested

  • Sanitation & BBP: Autoclave use, BBP, sharps disposal, cross-contamination prevention.
  • Anatomy & Physiology: Skin layers, wound healing phases, lymphatic system. PMU depth (1.5mm Dermis).
  • Contraindications: Diabetes, Keloids, Pregnancy, Accutane.
  • Color Theory & Pigments: Fitzpatrick scale, organic vs inorganic pigments, color correction.

Time Management Strategy

30 minutes for 50 questions = 36 seconds per question.

  • The 20-Minute First Pass: Answer what you know instantly. If it takes > 30s, Flag it (🚩) and move on.
  • The 5-Minute Review: Return to flagged questions. Use elimination.
  • Watch the Error Tracker: 20 errors is a hard fail. Trust your first instinct.

Prometric Center Tips

  • Read Carefully: Watch for "EXCEPT", "NOT", or "ALWAYS".
  • Sanitation is King: If asked about preventing infection, the answer almost always involves the Autoclave or single-use disposables.
  • Depth Matters: PMU is implanted into the upper reticular Dermis (~1.5mm). Deeper = tattoo. Shallower = fades instantly.
