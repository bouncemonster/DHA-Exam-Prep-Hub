Official DHA Exam Guidelines

About DHA Medical License

The Dubai Health Authority (DHA) is the regulatory body for healthcare professionals in Dubai, UAE. All medical practitioners must pass the DHA licensing examination to practice medicine in Dubai.

Exam Format

Format: Computer-based Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)

Language: English only

Duration: 30 minutes per block

Questions: 50 questions per block

Passing Score: 60% (minimum 30 correct answers)

Maximum Errors: 20 questions

Exam Topics & Blocks

  • Anatomy & Physiology: Human body systems, organ functions, cellular biology
  • Skin Disorders: Dermatological conditions, infections, treatments
  • Medical Procedures: Clinical techniques, diagnostic methods, treatment protocols
  • Sanitation & Hygiene: Infection control, sterilization, safety protocols
  • Professional Regulations: UAE healthcare laws, ethical standards, DHA requirements
  • Pharmacology: Drug classifications, mechanisms, contraindications
  • Pathology: Disease processes, diagnostic criteria, laboratory values
  • Clinical Practice: Patient care, diagnosis, treatment planning

Eligibility Requirements

  • Valid medical degree from recognized institution
  • Primary source verification of qualifications
  • Good standing certificate from previous practice locations
  • Valid passport and UAE residency (if applicable)
  • Completed DHA online application

Study Recommendations

  • Preparation Timeline: 6-8 weeks of dedicated study
  • Daily Study: 2-3 hours minimum
  • Practice Tests: Complete at least 10 full blocks before exam
  • Weak Areas: Identify and focus extra time on challenging topics
  • Time Management: Practice completing 50 questions in 30 minutes

Document Checklist

Ensure you have all required documents prepared before your DHA exam application and appointment.

Time Management Strategies

Exam Time Breakdown

Total Time: 30 minutes for 50 questions

Average per Question: 36 seconds

Recommended Pace: 30 seconds per question + 5 minutes review

Time Management Tips

First Pass Strategy (20 minutes)

Answer all questions you know confidently. Skip difficult questions and mark them for review. This ensures you capture all easy points first.

Second Pass Strategy (5 minutes)

Return to skipped questions. Use elimination method to narrow down options. Make educated guesses if necessary - there's no penalty for wrong answers.

Final Review (5 minutes)

Review marked questions. Check for any accidentally skipped questions. Verify you've answered all 50 questions.

Question Pacing Guide

  • Questions 1-10: Complete by 6 minutes (warm-up pace)
  • Questions 11-25: Complete by 12 minutes (steady pace)
  • Questions 26-40: Complete by 20 minutes (maintain pace)
  • Questions 41-50: Complete by 25 minutes (final push)
  • Last 5 minutes: Review and verify all answers

Warning Signs

  • Spending more than 2 minutes on a single question - skip and return later
  • Only 15 minutes left with more than 30 questions remaining - increase pace
  • Less than 5 minutes with unanswered questions - focus on completing all

Practice Timing

During your preparation, always practice with the actual time limit. Use the built-in timer in Study Mode to simulate real exam conditions. This helps you develop an internal clock and reduce time pressure anxiety.

Practical Tips for Exam Success

Study Strategies

Active Recall

Test yourself frequently rather than just reading. Use practice questions to identify weak areas and reinforce learning.

Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals. Study the same topic on Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, and Day 14 for better retention.

Focus on Weak Areas

Spend 70% of study time on topics where you score below 60%. Don't just practice what you already know well.

Exam Day Preparation

  • Night Before: Light review only. Get 7-8 hours of sleep. Avoid cramming.
  • Morning Of: Eat a balanced breakfast. Arrive 30 minutes early. Bring required documents.
  • At Test Center: Use restroom before exam. Keep water bottle nearby. Take deep breaths to calm nerves.

Anxiety Management

Breathing Technique

If feeling anxious, pause and breathe: Inhale for 4 counts, hold for 4, exhale for 4. Repeat 3 times to reset your nervous system.

Positive Visualization

Before exam, visualize yourself calmly answering questions and successfully passing. Mental rehearsal improves actual performance.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Reading too quickly and missing key words like "NOT" or "EXCEPT"
  • Changing correct answers during review (trust your first instinct unless certain)
  • Leaving questions unanswered (always guess if unsure)
  • Spending too long on difficult questions (skip and return)
  • Ignoring the clock (check time after every 10 questions)

Last-Minute Cramming Tips

  • Focus on high-yield topics: anatomy basics, common diseases, standard procedures
  • Review your previous error logs from practice tests
  • Memorize key numbers: normal lab values, drug dosages, vital signs
  • Don't try to learn new complex topics - reinforce what you know

Post-Exam Actions

  • Don't discuss answers with others immediately after - it increases anxiety
  • Note any topics you found particularly challenging for future reference
  • Wait for official results rather than trying to calculate your score
  • If you don't pass, review weak areas and reschedule within 30 days
